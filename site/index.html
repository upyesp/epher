A programmable, scriptable calculator

One calculation engine, four ways to use it — the command line, the terminal, the desktop, and an offline web app. Type expressions, save functions and scripts, graph the results in 2D or 3D, and keep everything between sessions. No account, no cloud: your work stays on your device.

Why epher

  • One download, four frontends

    The same epher program runs as a one-shot command, a REPL, a full-screen terminal UI, and a desktop app. Learn it once, use it anywhere.

  • A real language

    Variables, functions with recursion, loops, and scripts — with honest error messages. Newlines and `;` separate statements; both mean the same thing.

  • Exact when it matters

    Binary floats by default, with exact layers one call away: frac(1, 3), dec(0.1) + dec(0.2), and big(10 ^ 20).

  • Graphs in 2D and 3D

    Plot curves and surfaces, trace points, animate any constant with a play button, and orbit 3D views with touch or keyboard.

  • Private by design

    No accounts, no telemetry, no cloud. Everything is computed and stored on your device — the web app even works fully offline.

  • Speaks your language

    The interface ships in eight languages — English, Chinese, Hindi, Spanish, French, Arabic, German, and Portuguese — with full right-to-left support.
